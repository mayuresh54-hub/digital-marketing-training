About the Project

Digital Marketing for Small Businesses

Helping small businesses understand and use digital platforms effectively.

Why This Project?

Many small businesses have excellent products and services but may not know how to effectively promote them online.

This CEP project aims to provide simple, practical and easy-to-understand digital marketing training.

  • ✓ Improve digital marketing awareness.
  • ✓ Teach affordable online marketing techniques.
  • ✓ Help businesses build an online presence.
  • ✓ Increase customer engagement.

Who Can Benefit?

The training is designed for people who operate small or local businesses.

🏪 Local retail shops
🍰 Home-based businesses
🍽️ Restaurants and food businesses
🛠️ Local service providers
🎨 Artists and small creators

Introduction to Digital Marketing

Digital marketing means promoting products, services or businesses using digital channels such as websites, search engines, social media and email.

1. Build an Online Presence
Create a website and business profiles on relevant platforms.
2. Reach Customers
Use social media and search platforms to reach potential customers.
3. Create Useful Content
Share information that is useful and relevant to your audience.
4. Measure Results
Track engagement and other useful metrics.
